# PDF transparency

Transparency support is incomplete. The renderer handles device-RGB compositing,
the 16 standard blend modes, image alpha masks and isolated, non-knockout RGB
transparency Form XObjects. Other group configurations and graphics-state soft
masks are rejected rather than rendered as ordinary opaque forms.

## Isolated group Form XObjects

A form with Group subtype Transparency, I true and K false starts on a transparent
backdrop. Its members composite into an RGBA buffer. On entry, both alpha
constants reset to 1 and the blend mode resets to Normal. The finished group is
then painted once using the caller's nonstroking alpha and blend mode. The caller's
state is unchanged, and each invocation renders the group again.

This distinction matters when members overlap. Applying a group opacity to each
member separately darkens their overlap; applying it once to the completed group
does not. Nested groups, text, images, patterns and shading use the same group
buffer. Matrix transforms, form bounds and clipping still apply.

The supported blending space is DeviceRGB, either explicit, named through the
current ColorSpace resources, or inherited from the current RGB renderer.
Attributes may be indirect. A null optional entry is treated as absent; a null
Group entry leaves an ordinary form. DefaultRGB remapping is not implemented and
is rejected for these groups.

## Limits

- Non-isolated groups, knockout groups and non-RGB group blending spaces are not
  implemented. An omitted I flag defaults to false and is therefore unsupported.
- Page-group color-space selection, overprinting, alpha-as-shape behavior and
  full text-knockout semantics remain incomplete.
- Graphics-state soft-mask dictionaries are not yet supported. Existing image
  Mask/SMask handling is separate from those dictionaries.
- Group buffers store eight-bit RGBA. They are not floating-point or wide-gamut
  compositing surfaces.
- Simultaneously retained group buffers are limited to 64 MiB. Buffers currently
  span the full output page, so even a small group can reach that bound on a large
  page. Groups also share the form nesting, invocation and pixel-work budgets.
- A failed group is discarded before it is composited onto its parent. The
  allocation-failure tests check that its parent pixels and allocations remain
  unchanged on failure.

## Verification

Run:

    make test-pdf-groups
    python3 scripts/test_pdf_groups.py bin/test_pdf_pages --reference

The suite has 38 cases and 22 optional independent comparisons. It checks opacity
application, blend-state resets, nested groups, strokes, clipping, inherited and
indirect attributes, text, images, patterns, shading and rejected configurations.
All expected pixel images are analytic. Selected alpha comparisons allow one
RGB unit for intermediate eight-bit rounding; other comparisons are exact.

The bounding-box case has an analytic oracle only because the independent
rasterizers round group bounds differently. Other comparison fixtures avoid
edge ties so they test compositing rather than differing boundary conventions.
These checks do not establish general transparency or whole-document parity.

The isolated-opacity asset is generated by
`scripts/generate_pdf_group_fixture.py`. Its overlapping shapes must receive the
outer opacity once. The PDF allocation-failure suite has 1,352 checks, including
group allocation, malformed content, nesting and memory-budget failures.

## Specification

The group implementation follows [ISO 32000-1](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/pdfstandards/PDF32000_2008.pdf),
sections 11.4, 11.6.4.4 and 11.6.6, including the group attributes in Table 147.
Image masks, graphics-state soft masks, page groups and transparency Form XObjects
are distinct mechanisms; supporting one does not establish support for the others.
